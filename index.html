Who We Are

Blossom World Sr. Secondary School

An English Medium Co-educational Senior Secondary School set up to provide opportunities that empower young minds to become their own personalities, help them identify their inherent strengths, enable them to realize their true potential and, above all, to teach them to empathize and collaborate with others by celebrating differences and learning from them. The school is listed in Silchar (Uttarkrishnapur / Dhamalia area) and serves the local community with structured academic and co-curricular programmes.
